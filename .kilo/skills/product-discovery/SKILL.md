# Product Discovery

Clarify the user problem before choosing a solution.

1. Identify target users, current pain, desired outcome, constraints, and non-goals from available evidence.
2. Separate facts, assumptions, open questions, and decisions requiring the product owner.
3. Define a smallest valuable scope with observable acceptance criteria and a success metric or qualitative validation method.
4. Identify dependencies, privacy/security concerns, affected workflows, and alternatives worth presenting.
5. Produce a prioritized backlog: now, later, and explicitly out of scope.

Do not turn unknown business choices into implementation requirements. Record decisions and acceptance criteria in a form that implementation and QA can trace.
