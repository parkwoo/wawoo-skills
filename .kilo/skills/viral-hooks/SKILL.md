# Viral Hooks (popular content)

Create hooks that earn attention and **pay it off**, without deceiving readers.

## Hook formula

1. **Audience**: who stops scrolling, and why now?
2. **Tension**: a concrete pain, surprise, or contradiction.
3. **Promise**: a specific, deliverable payoff (what they will learn or get).
4. **Proof**: a number, example, or authority — only if true.
5. **Open loop**: a question or gap that the body resolves.

## Title / opening patterns

- Specific result ("How X cut Y by 40% — and the tradeoff")
- Contrarian but honest ("Why Z is overrated for most teams")
- Curiosity gap with a payoff ("The bug that only appeared at midnight")

## Structure

Hook → stakes → evidence → steps → objections → payoff → CTA.

## Ethics (do not)

- No false promises, fabricated numbers, or misleading titles.
- Do not exploit fear; state uncertainty.
- Match the title to the content (no bait-and-switch).

## Checklist

- [ ] One clear audience and promise
- [ ] Hook is specific, not generic
- [ ] Payoff is delivered in the body
- [ ] Claims are backed by evidence
- [ ] No clickbait or misinformation
